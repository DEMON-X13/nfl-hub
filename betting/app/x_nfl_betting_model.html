X NFL Betting Model

Downloads

Save the files you need, then upload them in the next section. Scores and team stats every week; roster and injury report weekly; the depth chart every few weeks.

Team stats Player stats Roster Injury report Depth chart
  • Scores and schedule (games.csv) grade your picks and the model's. Upload it first, or together with the others.
  • Team stats are each team's box score for every game played, and this is what moves the ratings. The file only exists once games have been played.
  • Player stats keep the quarterback ratings current and record who actually started each game. Weekly, with the team stats.
  • The roster answers "who is actually out": reserve and exempt designations mean the player cannot play. Small file, grab it weekly.
  • The injury report is practice participation. Official Out and Doubtful tags only appear late in the game week.
  • The depth chart identifies the starting quarterback. About 48MB, so re-download every few weeks rather than weekly.

Upload

Pick every file you downloaded in one go; each is recognised by its columns. Finished games are graded against the pick that was showing once both scores and team stats are in, ratings update, and the picks move to next week. Re-uploading is safe; processed games are skipped. Injury files are read in, but nothing is applied to the model until you click a suggestion.

Bet log

One line per week. Enter what you actually staked and what came back, and the chart above the table tracks your balance. Nothing here feeds the model, it is just your record.

Moneylines

The fetch button pulls published moneylines and spreads from the nflverse schedule file. For your own book’s numbers, upload a CSV with game_id, away_moneyline, home_moneyline. Payouts below are calculated from whatever is loaded.

Backup and reset

  • Save backup now writes everything to a JSON file: team ratings, graded results, your picks, bet log, loaded odds. This also happens automatically every time you upload results, so a graded week is never left only in the browser.
  • Import backup restores one of those files, or moves your season to another computer. It replaces your current state completely.
  • Rebuild ratings from Week 1 puts team ratings back to the preseason board and forgets which games were graded, but keeps your picks, bankroll, bets, odds and team news. Then re-upload the season files (games and team stats) from Week 1 and every game is graded again, against your kept picks, under the current model. Use it after a model correction mid-season.
  • Reset to preseason baseline throws away every result you have uploaded and rebuilds team ratings from the preseason numbers in this file. Your season starts over from Week 1.

Where backups land. They go to your browser's download folder. To keep them somewhere specific, point Chrome at it: Settings, Downloads, Location.

Why this matters: the app keeps your season in browser storage. Clearing cookies and site data, or "Clear browsing data" with site data ticked, erases it. A JSON backup restores everything, so keep one somewhere outside the browser.

When to reset: if the status line above says your saved ratings came from an older build, or if a bad upload has thrown the ratings off. After resetting, re-upload every week's files in order from Week 1 so the ratings rebuild correctly. Export a backup first, since reset cannot be undone.
